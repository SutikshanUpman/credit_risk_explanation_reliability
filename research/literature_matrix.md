## Positioning statement

The project does **not** claim to be the first study of SHAP explanation stability in credit risk. Its contribution is a compact empirical integration of borrower-level rank agreement, signed-attribution variability, and a qualified model-sensitivity comparison under paired seed and positive-class loss-weighting conditions. It complements, rather than supersedes, the 2025 credit-risk stability studies above. The fixed dataset, split, model family, and perturbation design limit the scope of that contribution.
